Integrations

Connect the store to other apps: Zapier, Make, n8n, Google Sheets, your ERP or warehouse system. They get a message the moment something happens.

Accounting export

Download sales as invoices and import them into Xero (Business > Invoices > Import) or QuickBooks Online (Settings > Import data > Invoices). Cancelled and unpaid orders are left out.

Webhooks

Paste the address your app gives you (in Zapier: Webhooks by Zapier > Catch Hook; in Make: Webhooks > Custom webhook). Each message is JSON, signed with X-Lumen-Signature: sha256=… (HMAC of the body with the secret). Failed messages are retried for 12 hours.
