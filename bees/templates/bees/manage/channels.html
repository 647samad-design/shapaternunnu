Google Shopping

Free listings, Shopping ads, and Google's AI shopping in Search and Gemini.

  1. Open Google Merchant Center and verify your website.
  2. Products → Add products → Add from a file → Enter a link to your file.
  3. Paste the address, choose a daily fetch and save.

Facebook & Instagram

Shops on Facebook and Instagram, and catalog ads.

  1. Open Meta Commerce Manager and create a catalog.
  2. Data sources → Data feed → Scheduled feed.
  3. Paste the address and pick a daily update.

TikTok Shop

TikTok catalog for shoppable videos and ads.

  1. In TikTok Ads Manager open Assets → Catalogs.
  2. Add products → Data feed → Scheduled.
  3. Paste the same address as Facebook.
