Mobile app

Your store already works as an app: on any phone shoppers can tap "Add to Home screen". To be on Google Play as well, package it in about 15 minutes - no coding, and every change to your store shows up in the app instantly.

Publish on Google Play

  1. Create a Google Play developer account at play.google.com/console (one-time fee of US$25).
  2. Open PWABuilder (pwabuilder.com), enter {{ site }} and press Start. It reads your app details from {{ manifest }}.
  3. Package for Android: choose "Google Play", set the package name (e.g. com.yourstore.app) and download. Keep the signing key file safe - you need it for every update.
  4. Upload the .aab file to Play Console > Create app > Production, add screenshots and a description.
  5. Link the app to your site: copy the SHA-256 fingerprint from Play Console > App integrity > App signing, and paste it with the package name under Keys & passwords > Android app.
  6. Add the Play Store link there too - a "Get the app" badge then appears in your store's footer.
